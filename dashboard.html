What gets pulled from each site

For every URL, ProScouter checks the homepage first, then falls back to the pages you enabled on the left:

  1. Homepage & footer
  2. Contact / About page
  3. Privacy Policy page
  4. Checkout / Terms page

Sites are crawled 15 at a time. A run of 10,000 URLs typically finishes in well under an hour, depending on how many sites are slow to respond.
